01 · Profile

I build what I wish already existed.

Most of my time outside class goes into three overlapping interests: teaching robots to make decisions, doing math for the sake of it, and research at the seam between them.

Right now that means researching machine learning at MIT and UC Santa Cruz, running RIHS Math Club and Apex STEM — which I founded — and serving as Treasurer of VEX Robotics and Captain of FTC Team 36541, while mentoring rookie teammates and writing free math curriculum.

I like problems that stay interesting after the first correct answer.
