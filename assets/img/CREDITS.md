# Image credits

The site ships no photographs or rendered posters. Every picture on the page is
drawn live: the hero's field in WebGL (`assets/js/terrain.js`), the two research
demos in Three.js (`assets/js/demos.js`), and the write-up's flyby on a 2D canvas
(`assets/js/flyby.js`).

The NASA Earth composite and the card posters used by earlier versions remain in
the repository history.
